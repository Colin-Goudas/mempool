Understanding transaction fees

These are live fee-rate estimates from the provider’s observed Bitcoin queue. The small chevron connects this panel to the projected blocks above; it does not track a particular transaction.

Economy: lower priority, with no fixed deadline. Standard: targets roughly three blocks. Fast: targets the next block. New demand, related transactions and irregular block times can change the wait.

The examples below use a 140-vB payment, rounded up to whole satoshis and converted at the current price. Your actual fee depends on transaction size. Wallet or exchange charges may differ.
